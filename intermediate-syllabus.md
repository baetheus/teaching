# 7-Session Intermediate Ceramics

This ceramics course covers both technical skills for throwing and aesthetic
analysis in the area of pottery. We will cover at least one set a fundamental
throwing skills for weights up to 5 lbs and matbe more with a focus on
efficiency. We will also cover analytical skills that will help you identify
areas for improvement in your own throwing as well as increase your ability to
plan your work in advance. Lastly, we set aside time for special techniques that
students in the class wish to pursue. Optionally, there will be regular sessions
where we will learn how to critique work using observational skills.

---

## Goals

### Fundamentals
1. Improve 1 lb throwing speed to under 5 minutes
2. Introduce tall throwing techniques for 5+ lbs
3. Introduce analytical skillset

### Class-Specific
4. Student choice: shapes & specialties (Examples)
    a. Lids
    b. Spouts
    c. Handles
    
5. *(Optional)* Introduce Aesthetic Critique

---

## Techniques

### Fundamental Techniques
1. Reducing water usage
2. Centering
3. Opening tall instead of short
4. How to pull: hand position, tool/pot size ratio
3. Collaring with both hands
4. Rib shaping

### Tall Techniques
1. Centering to the top
2. Thick wall pulls
3. Collar pulls
4. Big pulls
5. Planning pots:
  - 2/3 for Body, 1/3 for Neck
  - Shaping in early pulls
  - Cleaning bowl bottoms

---

## Analysis

1. Observation: the root of analysis - when to do it?
2. Picking out elements of a curve: Endpoints, Inflections, 
3. Shape, color, surface observations
4. Regulated vs. free workmanship; the workmanship of risk vs. certainty
5. Poorly made vs well made work
6. Rough vs precise work
7. Joining rough and precise work

---

## Critique *(Optional)*

1. How to create a rubric
2. How to analyze according to a rubric
3. Talking about feel

---

## Exercises

### Fundamental Exercises
1. No sponge throwing — Only allowed to add water, not remove. Repeat until no
   puddles in your finished work.
2. Slip only: center piece, knock off, recenter. Only allowed water at the
   beginning.
3. Compress base fist, raising walls in process.
4. Small plates off hump (collar/wire off) to practice right hand control.
5. Small cups off hump (all skills) to pracice collar pulls

### Tall Exercises
1. General weight progression (1 shape, sized up between weights):
    - 3 lb. vases, bottles
    - 4 lb. vases, bottled
    - 5 lb. vases, bottled
2. Vase to plate to vase
4. Wire off buttons

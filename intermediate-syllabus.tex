\sub{Description}

This ceramics throwing course intends to further both technical skills
and to impart clear analytical practices for iterating on and refining
work.  The core technical skills center on throwing efficiency and
ergonomics but include explorations of alternative throwing systems, larger
throwing, throwing off the hump, and techniques off the wheel as well.

\sub{Calendar}

\begin{multicols}{2}

	\split{Session 1}{Centering, Opening / Drawing}{9/28 7:30-9:30pm}
	\begin{itemize}
		\item \textbf{Drawing}: Using the paper sheets passed out in class, draw a
		      mug silhouette inside the 4x5 inch box. Cut out the better half of your
		      silhouette and paste it onto a piece of cardboard. Then cut out the mug
		      portion of your drawing to make a form tool.
		\item \textbf{Opening Short}: Demo. Center clay into a puck, dig down about 1 cm
		      and open into short walls, dig down another 1 cm and open into short walls
		      again, continue until you get to the wheel head. Focus on cleanly
		      blending the opened clay into the existing walls.
		\item \textbf{Opening Tall}: Demo.Center clay into a flat topped cone. Slowly
		      drill down into the center with your left thumb while lightly controlling
		      the walls splaying out with your right hand. Notice how it is easier when
		      you move more slowly and pay attention to clay stickiness.
		\item \textbf{Centering Practice}: We cover centering short and tall. For practice,
		      center 1.5 to 3lbs, knock off center, recenter without adding water,
		      repeat until failure. Focus on how the stickiness of the clay changes with
		      less water and start over after 10-20 recenters.
	\end{itemize}

	\split{Session 2}{Pulling, Shaping / Timing}{10/5 7:30-9:30pm}
	\begin{itemize}
		\item \textbf{Pulling}: Demo of Knuckle Pull, Collar Pull, Compression Pull.
		\item \textbf{Shaping}: Demo the fundamentals of using ribs for shaping.
		\item \textbf{Timing Practice}: Prepare 10 balls of clay each weighing ~1lb, start a
		      five minute timer with the intention to throw the mug shape made in
		      Session 1, when the time goes off wire off the piece even if it is not
		      finished and cut it in half to check wall thickness. Rest for 1ish minute
		      between pots but don't do any analysis until all 10 balls have been
		      thrown. Notice how throwing is easier as you focus only on throwing
		      techniques and not on what the outcome is.
	\end{itemize}

	\split{Session 3}{Trimming, Texture, and Handles}{10/12 7:30-9:30pm}
	\begin{itemize}
		\item \textbf{Trimming}: Demo using counter friction to trim quickly.
		\item \textbf{Texture}: Demo using various tools to add texture and shape to
		      leatherhard work.
		\item \textbf{Handles}: Demo pulling handles and sculpting handles.
	\end{itemize}

	\columnbreak

	\split{Session 4}{Throwing Larger}{10/19 7:30-9:30pm}
	\begin{itemize}
		\item \textbf{Centering}: Demo centering 3 to 5lbs of clay using a medium speed
		      and less strength using the top and bottom technique.
		\item \textbf{Opening}: Demo using the tall opening method. Drill down until the
		      left hand is full of clay. If there is still more clay to go, use the left
		      fist to punch down into the clay and compress the base. Lastly, use the
		      flat outside of the left thumb and the right palm to lightly compress and
		      even the walls.
		\item \textbf{Big Pulls}: Demo thick pulls with the flat thumb and palm of
		      outside hand. Collar pulls to get initial height. Big knuckle pulls for
		      initial height.
		\item \textbf{Practice Larger}: Untimmed practice of the large throwing
		      techniques.
	\end{itemize}

	\split{Session 5}{The Hump}{10/26 7:30-9:30pm}
	\begin{itemize}
		\item \textbf{Buttons}: Demo throwing small buttons off the hump to exercise
		      wiring off with string.
		\item \textbf{Small Plates}: Demo throwing small plates off the hump to
		      exercise a basic compression pull without shaping.
		\item \textbf{Small Bowls}: Demo throwing small bowls off the hump to
		      exercise the compression pull with shaping.
		\item \textbf{Mugs}: Once some small plates and bowls have been thrown try
		      making a mug that matches your form from day 1.
		\item \textbf{Teapots}: Demo of throwing all the necessary parts for a
		      teapot off the hump.
	\end{itemize}

	\split{Session 6}{Glazing}{11/2 7:30-9:30pm}
	\begin{itemize}
		\item \textbf{Underglaze}: Demo using underglaze directly with sgrafitto.
		\item \textbf{Dipping Glaze}: Demo dipping cups, bowls, and larger vases.
		\item \textbf{Resists}: Demo using resists over shino.
	\end{itemize}

	\split{Session 7}{Review}{11/9 7:30-9:30pm}
	At this point we have covered many fundamental topics which will be reviewed.
	Students are encouraged to ask for clarification or deeper discussion of the
	topics that most interest them. We will also cover techniques for finding and
	developing new ideas before throwing, how to build a rubric for assessing your
	own work without relying on vibes, and a couple of starting points for further
	exploration.


\end{multicols}
